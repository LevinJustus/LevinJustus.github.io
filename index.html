Motor drives / 2026

Research on Freewheeling Optimization Method for Permanent Magnet Claw-Pole Stepper Motor Drive Circuits

Accepted · PSPE 2026

Liang Shen, Yuting Zou, Luqing Jia, Xinyi Huang

5th International Conference on Power Systems and Power Engineering · Forthcoming

Optimizing the freewheeling path to accelerate winding-current decay and improve commutation behavior without changing the motor structure.

+My contributions & workflowExplore

Second author · Principal contributor

  • Investigated how winding coupling, freewheeling current, and rotation-induced EMF affect the commutation process.
  • Worked on the drive-circuit model and resistor-based freewheeling optimization, supported by Maxwell 3D and PSIM analysis.
  • Compared current-decay and dynamic-torque behavior at different excitation rates, and prepared the PSPE presentation.
  1. 01Coupled windings
  2. 02Freewheeling model
  3. 03Resistor selection
  4. 04Torque comparison

Accepted for publication. The current validation is theoretical and simulation-based; practical-platform experiments remain future work.
