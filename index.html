About

I am an undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering and Automation at the School of Transportation and Electrical Engineering, Dalian University of Science and Technology. My research interests include motor control, intelligent control, and computer vision.

I am advised by Liang Shen (申亮), a Ph.D. student at Dalian Jiaotong University, and Wei Dong (董玮), Dean of the School of Transportation and Electrical Engineering at my university. My work spans adaptive and iterative learning control for electric motors, motor drive circuits, and vision-based rail surface inspection.
